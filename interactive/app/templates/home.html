HealthPAL

A personal library of clinic visit recordings to help patients and caregivers manage care.

This site demonstrates clinician-facing tools built on visit recordings. It analyzes transcripts you upload and is not the real HealthPAL application.

About

HealthPAL empowers patients and caregivers to make recordings of clinic visits, store them securely, and easily navigate the audio or video recordings. Once a recording is made, patients can share their recordings with family members or caregivers, make their own notes in-app, and play back their recordings to hear any information they may have missed at the time of recording. The clinician tools in this demo show how features extracted from a visit, such as who is speaking, the kinds of questions asked, and repetition, can give clinicians context alongside the recording.
